Number Sense

Place value, factors, multiples, primes and negative numbers.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

prime
A number greater than 1 with exactly two factors.
Example: 37 is prime because only 1 and 37 divide exactly.
factor
A number that divides into another number exactly.
Example: 6 is a factor of 36.
integer
A whole number including negatives and zero.
Example: -4, 0 and 12 are integers.

Core explanation

Glossary with definitions and examples

fraction
A way of showing part of a whole.
Example: 3/5 means 3 equal parts out of 5.
percentage
A fraction out of 100.
Example: 60% means 60 out of 100.
equivalent
Different forms with the same value.
Example: 1/2 and 0.5 are equivalent.

Ratio and Proportion

Share amounts, scale recipes, compare rates and use the unitary method.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

ratio
A comparison between quantities.
Example: 3:5 means 3 parts to 5 parts.
unitary method
Find one part first, then scale up.
Example: If 4 pens cost £2, one pen costs 50p.
scale factor
A multiplier used to enlarge or reduce.
Example: A scale factor of 2 doubles every length.

Linear Graphs

Coordinates, gradients, intercepts and straight-line equations.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

gradient
The steepness of a line.
Example: A line rising 2 for every 1 across has gradient 2.
intercept
Where a graph crosses an axis.
Example: In y=2x+3, the y-intercept is 3.
coordinate
A position written as x,y.
Example: The point (2,5) has x=2 and y=5.

Calculus

Differentiation, integration and rates of change.

Core explanation

A strong answer defines the key concept, applies the skill, uses an example, and explains why the reasoning is accurate.

Glossary with definitions and examples

derivative
The rate of change of a function.
Example: The derivative of x² is 2x.
integral
The reverse of differentiation or area under a curve.
Example: Integrating speed gives distance.
stationary point
Where the gradient is zero.
Example: A maximum or minimum can be a stationary point.

Formula bank and misconception busters

Gradient

The rate of change of a straight line, calculated as change in y divided by change in x.

Intercept

The point where a graph crosses an axis.

Ratio

A comparison of quantities in the same units.

Proportion

A relationship where one quantity changes in relation to another.

Discriminant

The value b² - 4ac used to determine the number of real roots of a quadratic.

Median

The middle value when data are placed in order.

Probability

A measure of likelihood between 0 and 1.

Vector

A quantity with magnitude and direction.
